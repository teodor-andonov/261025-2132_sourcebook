# 2026-10-27 — Bulk tag development cycle

Implemented: catalog multi-selection; Ctrl/Cmd+A scoped to filtered folder; nested hashtag Add/Remove on up to 250 selected items; stable-ID planning; catalog-only preflight, verified sequential writes, conflict and partial-failure reporting; pure and simulated filesystem tests.

Safety decisions: never rename/move/write ordinary source files; preserve unrelated tags and notes; refuse unknown tag IDs and ambiguous paths; preflight all selected records; recheck taxonomy and record before each write. Catalog JSON writes are **not** multi-file atomic and do not guarantee protection against races between final check and write.

Remaining: physical Windows/Android tests, transactional recovery, catalog-wide filtering, saved Views and cross-root canonicalization. Screenshots and Pages publishing remain manual only.
